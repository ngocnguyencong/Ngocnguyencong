# src/entitlements/presentation.ts

> Last updated: 2026-10-01 | Protection: STANDARD

## Purpose

Builds neutral, server-verified workspace entitlement copy for mobile billing.

The presentation does not determine billing management. It describes an active
server-verified entitlement, reports a recorded expiration date when present,
and never infers renewal or lifetime status from that date.
