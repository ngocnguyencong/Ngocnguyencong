# src/kit/blocks/marketing/testimonial-grid.module.css

> Last updated: 2026-09-28 | Protection: STANDARD

## Purpose

Section heading, grid, quote, identity, and avatar styling for the copyable testimonial block.

## What It Does

Four cards use a two-column natural-height layout, collapsing to one column in narrow containers or viewports; other counts keep the original grid. Heading, spacing, identity, and five-star rows use active theme tokens. `--space-room` and `--radius-surface` follow inherited style presets. Outline stars remain visible for 4/5 and 2/5 submissions.

## Important Notes

- Locked Supastack marketing keeps its sans heading. Inherit mode uses the chosen editorial, geometric, or monospace font preset.

2026-10-06: Colored foregrounds use `--accent-text` with an accent fallback. Keep action fills, borders, and control indicators on `--accent`; light-palette text must meet 4.5:1 on plain and tinted surfaces.
