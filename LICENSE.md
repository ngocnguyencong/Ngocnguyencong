# Supastack License

A supastack license is a one-time purchase that grants ongoing access to this
codebase and the rights described below. It covers use of the kit source —
products you build on top of it keep their own terms, which remain yours to set.

## The license grant

supastack keeps ownership of the codebase and grants each licensee an ongoing,
non-exclusive, worldwide license to use the code to create end products.

## What you can do

The license is deliberately broad inside one boundary: the products you ship
are yours.

- Build and ship as many end products as you like — for yourself, your
  company, or your clients.
- Sell the products you build and charge your own users however you choose.
- Buy on a client's behalf as a freelancer or agency, with one seat per
  developer who needs codebase access.
- Modify and extend the code inside your own private repositories.

## What you can't do

The codebase itself is the licensed product — it can't become something you
pass on.

- Sell, resell, redistribute, or publicly share the codebase itself —
  including as a starter kit, template, or repackaged boilerplate.
- Share your license or codebase access with anyone beyond your purchased
  seats.
- Publish the source in a public repository without written permission.

## Seats and revocation

Each developer with codebase access needs their own seat; the end users of
products you ship never count as seats. Breaking these terms can revoke the
license with no refund, and repository access is removed.

## Questions

If you are unsure how the license applies to your case — agency work, a client
handover, or something else — ask through the contact form on the supastack
website.
